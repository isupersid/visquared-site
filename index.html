Know before you start

You bring the service. The app is the client.

No channels included

STB for webOS does not provide, sell, host, or endorse content, channel lists, or portal subscriptions.

Your lawful access

You must supply a compatible service and have all lawful rights and permissions needed to access it. Your provider controls where its portal and content are available.

Privacy by design

Useful diagnostics, without viewing habits

On first run, you explicitly choose whether to share Basic diagnostics. Detailed telemetry is off by default, and telemetry can be turned off in the app.

We do not collect account credentials, content titles, search terms, raw LGUDID values, or stream URLs through telemetry.
